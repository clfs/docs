# Apple crisp

## Ingredients

Filling:

- 1.5 lbs Granny Smith apples, whole
- 1.5 lbs sweet apples, whole
- 1/4 cup liquor
- 106g light brown sugar
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp ginger
- 20g AP flour
- 1/4 tsp salt

Topping:

- 180g AP flour
- 90g oats
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 284g light brown sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 16 tbsp butter, cold

## Steps

1. Preheat 350°F.
2. Grease a 9" square non-stick metal pan.
3. Peel the apples, core each apple into 8 slices, then cut each slice into 3 chunks.
4. Toss the apples in a bowl with the rest of the filling ingredients.
5. Spread the filling in the pan.
6. Whisk together the topping ingredients in a bowl, **except** butter and oats.
7. Mix pats of cold butter into the topping. Use two forks to "crumble" in the butter.
8. Mix oats into the topping.
9. Spread the topping over the filling.
10. Bake for at least 60 minutes, with a sheet pan underneath to catch spills.
11. Let cool for 20 minutes.

## Notes

Based on:

- <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-apple-crisp-recipe>
- <https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/apple-crisp/>

Mix oats into the topping last, so that crumbling the butter doesn't shred the oats.

I don't know whether powdered ginger or freshly grated ginger is better here.

Sweet apples:

- Pink Cripps: Good
- Honeycrisp: OK

Liquors:

- Bacardi Gold: Great
- Buffalo Trace: OK
